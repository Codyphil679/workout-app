# How to install LiftLog on your Android phone

Takes about 2 minutes. You need an **Android phone running Android 8.0 or newer**. iPhones can't run this app.

LiftLog isn't on the Google Play Store, so you install it straight from a file. Your phone will show a few safety warnings along the way. That's normal for apps from outside the Play Store.

---

## Step 1: Download the app

1. On your phone, open this link in **Chrome**:
   **https://github.com/Codyphil679/workout-app/releases/latest**
2. Scroll down to **Assets** and tap the file ending in **`.apk`** (for example `LiftLog-1.0.0.apk`).
3. If Chrome warns *"This type of file can harm your device"*, tap **Download anyway**.

## Step 2: Open the file

1. When the download finishes, tap **Open** at the bottom of the screen.
   - Missed it? Open the **Files** app (or **My Files** on Samsung) → **Downloads**, then tap the `.apk` file.

## Step 3: Allow Chrome to install apps (one time only)

The first time, Android blocks the install and says something like *"For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source."*

1. Tap **Settings** in that message.
2. Turn on **Allow from this source**.
3. Tap the **back arrow** to return to the installer.

> **Samsung phones:** if you see a message about **Auto Blocker**, go to **Settings → Security and privacy → Auto Blocker**, turn it **off**, then go back to Step 2. You can turn it back on after installing.

## Step 4: Install

1. Tap **Install**.
2. Google Play Protect may show a warning like *"Unsafe app blocked"* or *"Unknown developer"*:
   - Tap **More details** → **Install anyway**.
   - If it offers **Scan app**, you can tap that. It'll scan the app, then let you install.
3. Tap **Open** when it's done.

## Step 5: First launch

1. When it asks **"Allow LiftLog to send you notifications?"**, tap **Allow**.
   This is how the rest timer buzzes you when your rest is over while your phone is in your pocket or the screen is off.
2. You're in. To get started:
   - Go to **Programs** and tap a **starter template** (e.g. *Push / Pull / Legs*), or build your own.
   - Go to **Train** and tap **Start workout**.
   - Enter your weight and reps, then tap the **✓**. The rest timer starts automatically.

---

## Keep your data safe

Everything is stored **only on your phone**, with no account and no cloud.

- Back up now and then: **Settings → Back up everything**, and save the file to Google Drive.
- **Uninstalling the app deletes your workouts** unless you've made a backup.

## Installing an update later

1. Download the new `.apk` from the same link and install it the same way. Your workouts stay.
2. If your phone says **"App not installed"** or **"package conflicts with an existing package"**:
   1. In the old app: **Settings → Back up everything** and save the file.
   2. Uninstall LiftLog.
   3. Install the new version.
   4. In the new app: **Settings → Restore from backup** and pick your file.

## Something not working?

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| The download link shows no `.apk` file | Ask Cody to check that the release has the APK attached. |
| "App not installed" | Make sure you have Android 8.0 or newer (**Settings → About phone → Android version**). If it's an update, see *Installing an update* above. |
| Can't find the downloaded file | Open the **Files** / **My Files** app → **Downloads**. |
| Rest timer doesn't buzz | **Settings → Apps → LiftLog → Notifications** → turn them on. |
